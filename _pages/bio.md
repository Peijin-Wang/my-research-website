Peijin Wang is a PhD candidate in Biostatistics at Duke University, advised by Dr. Hwanhee Hong. Peijin's research develops Bayesian and causal inference methods for clinical trials, with an emphasis on incorporating external control data into randomized studies.

Current work focuses on dynamic borrowing, hybrid controlled trials, and the use of real-world data and real-world evidence in drug development. This methodological and collaborative research spans chronic lung allograft dysfunction, atrial fibrillation, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, and other clinical settings where efficient and interpretable evidence generation is especially important.

Peijin earned an MS in Biostatistics from Duke University and a BS in Statistics from Central South University. She expects to complete her PhD in May 2027.
